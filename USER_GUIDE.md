# NewsFindr - End User Guide

Welcome to **NewsFindr**, your personalized AI-powered news aggregation platform!

---

## 🎯 What is NewsFindr?

NewsFindr is an intelligent news aggregation application that:
- 📚 Learns your interests from your profile
- 🤖 Uses AI to expand your search topics
- 📰 Finds relevant news articles from Google News
- ✨ Summarizes key findings for quick reading
- 💡 Provides credible source verification

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Accessing the App

**Live App**: https://newsfindr.streamlit.app

Simply open the link in your web browser. No installation or account creation needed!

---

## 📖 How to Use NewsFindr

### Step 1: Select Your Profile

1. Open the app
2. Look for **"Select User Email"** dropdown
3. Choose your email address
   - Your profile contains your predefined interests
   - Example: `alice.6eb33c45-5@gmail.com`

### Step 2: Enter Your News Query

1. In the **"Enter News Query"** field, type what you want to read about
2. Examples:
   - `artificial intelligence`
   - `technology news`
   - `business`
   - `sports`
   - `climate change`

### Step 3: Get Your News Summary

1. Click the button or press Enter
2. Wait for processing (usually 5-10 seconds)
3. Your personalized news summary appears below!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why do some queries return "no credible sources"?

**A**: This happens when:
- The topic has very few recent news articles
- All available articles come from low-credibility sources
- Try a broader search term (e.g., "technology" instead of "obscure AI startup")

### Q: Can I search any topic?

**A**: Yes! Your profile interests are just defaults. You can search:
- ✅ Any news topic
- ✅ Any current event
- ✅ Any subject area
- The app finds news for any legitimate query

### Q: How often are the news sources updated?

**A**: NewsFindr pulls from Google News RSS feeds which update:
- Multiple times per day for major news
- Hourly for trending topics
- Fresh searches always get the latest available articles

### Q: What if I get an error?

**A**: Common solutions:
1. **Refresh the page** (Ctrl+F5)
2. **Try a simpler query** (e.g., "news" instead of complex phrases)
3. **Wait 30 seconds** and try again (server might be busy)
4. **Check your internet connection**

### Q: Can I change my interests/profile?

**A**: Currently, profiles are pre-configured. Contact your administrator to update your interests.

### Q: Is my data private?

**A**: 
- ✅ No personal data is stored on external servers
- ✅ Queries are processed locally
- ✅ News results are from public Google News feeds
- ✅ Your email is only used to load your profile

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "Processing..." never completes
**Solution**: 
- Wait up to 30 seconds
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+F5)
- Try again with a simpler query

### Issue: Same results every time
**Solution**:
- This is normal - news updates throughout the day
- Search again in a few hours for fresh results
- Try a different query term

### Issue: "I couldn't find any credible news sources"
**Solution**:
- Try a broader query (e.g., "news" instead of specific topic)
- Try a different topic from your interests
- This query genuinely has no recent news - try tomorrow

### Issue: App is slow/laggy
**Solution**:
- Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- Try a different browser
- Close other tabs using heavy resources
- Peak hours (9-5 workdays) may be slower
